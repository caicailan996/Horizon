### [Apple’s UK ADP withdrawal creates two-tier iCloud encryption](https://macanorak.com/two-tier-encryption-in-the-uk/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple has withdrawn Advanced Data Protection \(ADP\) for UK iCloud users in response to government surveillance orders, creating a two-tier encryption system. UK users lose ADP’s end-to-end encryption for the additional iCloud categories it covered, including iCloud Backup, Photos, Notes, and iCloud Drive, which revert to Standard Data Protection where Apple holds the keys. The 14 categories already encrypted by default, such as iCloud Keychain and Health, remain end-to-end encrypted.

hackernews · ReturnoftheHack · Sep 24, 10:39 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49828731)

**「Background」** Apple&\#x27;s Advanced Data Protection \(ADP\) is an opt-in end-to-end encryption tier for iCloud that extends protection beyond the 14 categories encrypted by default. In response to a UK government Technical Capability Notice \(TCN\) under the Investigatory Powers Act 2016, Apple withdrew ADP for UK users rather than build an encryption backdoor, triggering closed-door Tribunal proceedings and diplomatic pushback from the United States.

**「Impact」** UK users who rely on iCloud for end-to-end encrypted backups of sensitive data should now treat reverted categories like Photos, Notes, and iCloud Drive as accessible to Apple under lawful legal process, and should consider alternative encrypted storage for data that must remain private.

**「Community discussion」** Commenters split over whether the withdrawal was a defensible middle path or a retreat: egorfine argued Apple has lost the nerve it showed in 2015, palmotea framed it as avoiding a backdoor by removing the feature, and spr-alex cautioned that even default end-to-end encrypted categories may be exposed in common use. Hasz said he had bought Apple partly because of its earlier FBI stance and hoped Apple would stop selling to the UK government.

### [OpenAI Releases MentalHealthBench for Mental-Health AI Evaluation](https://openai.com/zh-Hans-CN/index/introducing-mentalhealthbench/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

OpenAI released MentalHealthBench, an open benchmark for evaluating AI responses in real-world mental-health conversations, developed with more than 80 licensed mental-health experts from 22 countries. The benchmark measures behaviors such as safety, gathering background information, preserving user autonomy, and providing actionable advice across scenarios involving adults, adolescents, caregivers, and clinicians. The results show steady progress in AI handling of mental-health concerns, though the release cautions that ChatGPT cannot replace professional treatment.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 24, 06:00

**「Background」** MentalHealthBench is a dataset of 1,215 synthetic, alternating mental-health conversations that mirror scenarios observed in real ChatGPT usage, according to OpenAI&\#x27;s accompanying technical report. The release also includes published model results—such as GPT-6 Astra scoring 57.3 and Claude Opus 5.5 scoring 52.4—making it a measurable evaluation suite rather than only a set of guidelines.

**「Impact」** Developers and researchers evaluating mental-health AI now have an expert-built, open set of scenarios and metrics to assess model behavior, including safety and user autonomy. The release also warns that ChatGPT responses should not be treated as a substitute for professional therapy, a limitation that should guide how such tools are positioned and deployed.
