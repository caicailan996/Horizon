### [OpenAI Announces Apparent ETH Refutation and Yang–Mills Proof](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/) ⭐️ 10.0/10

OpenAI announced a collaboration with academic communities that produced new results in mathematics, computer science, and physics, including an apparent refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis \(ETH\) and progress on Yang–Mills existence. The complexity result is a deterministic, polynomial-space algorithm that exactly counts satisfying assignments of an arbitrary k-CNF formula for fixed k ≥ 2, with runtime roughly 2^\{n/\(log n\)^\{1/2 + o\(1\)\}\} — still superpolynomial but subexponential. The physics result reportedly includes a Lean-formalized proof of Yang–Mills existence and a subsequent proof for QCD. These are announced findings and are not yet independently verified.

hackernews · jonluca · Oct 26, 21:35 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50251344)

**「Background」** The Exponential Time Hypothesis \(ETH\) is the long-standing conjecture that solving k-SAT requires time exponential in the number of variables; it is weaker than P ≠ NP but underpins many hardness results in parameterized complexity. Yang–Mills existence and the mass gap is a Clay Mathematics Institute Millennium Prize Problem asking for a rigorous mathematical foundation for the quantum field theories behind fundamental forces. OpenAI&\#x27;s announcement follows its October 16 &\#x27;Rosalind Academic Plan,&\#x27; which gave academic labs sponsored access to its GPT-Rosalind AI research assistant for scientific work, setting the stage for the academic collaborations described here.

**「Impact」** If the algorithm is verified, complexity researchers will need to re-evaluate the many hardness and lower-bound results that assume ETH, though commenters note the result does not show NP is subexponential and does not immediately imply P=NP.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters widely express disbelief, describing ETH as a long-standing hypothesis commonly used in parameterized complexity. One clarifies that the new runtime, while subexponential, is still superpolynomial and does not imply NP is subexponential; another asks whether a false ETH should shift priors toward P=NP.

### [CPython drops experimental JIT after PEP 836 is withdrawn](https://lwn.net/Articles/1102102/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

CPython&\#x27;s experimental just-in-time \(JIT\) compiler, introduced in the 2024 Python 3.13 release, is being abandoned: the Python steering council paused the project in June, and the JIT maintainers have now withdrawn PEP 836, the proposal intended to address council concerns about maintenance, compatibility, success metrics, and completion timeline. With the PEP withdrawn, development on the experimental JIT in the main branch has come to an end rather than progressing toward a supported feature.

rss · LWN.net · Oct 26, 15:45

**「Background」** CPython added an experimental JIT compiler as a new capability in Python 3.13, allowing the interpreter to generate native machine code at runtime. The steering council paused that work until an accepted Python Enhancement Proposal could define how the JIT would be supported, including maintenance and compatibility plans, clear success metrics, and a timeline for completion; PEP 836 was created to meet that requirement before being withdrawn.

**「Impact」** There is currently no accepted path for a supported JIT compiler in CPython, so developers who benchmarked or adopted Python 3.13&\#x27;s experimental JIT for performance should plan for it to remain experimental or be removed instead of being stabilized in future releases.

**「Impact」** Developers contributing new leaf components to systemd can now use Rust in the main tree, but core components that other parts depend on will remain in C until the dynamic-library blockers are addressed.

### [PBT Does Not Speed Up Grokking, But Can Escape Hyperparameter Dead Zones](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xfqhfc/pbt_doesnt_make_grokking_faster_but_it_can_escape/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

In a modular addition grokking experiment \(mod 97, 35% train split, small MLP, 20 Population-Based Training members for 20k full-batch steps\), PBT was slower than an equal-compute grid: the best fixed setting reached 95% validation accuracy at 317 ± 29 steps, while PBT took 2667 ± 2244 steps. However, PBT escaped a dead hyperparameter zone where no fixed setting grokked, succeeding 3/3 runs with fast selection \(~6850 steps\), compared with 0/3 for fixed or slow PBT. Replaying the winning learning-rate/weight-decay schedule without weight copying reached 95% in 55/60 fresh runs, against 0/60 for constant knobs at the schedule&\#x27;s geometric mean. The author concludes PBT is a rescue tool for bad ranges, not a speed-up, with caveats: one task, one model, one split, and p-values often above 0.05.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Virtual-Message-9739 · Oct 26, 07:48

**「Background」** Grokking is a deep learning phenomenon in which a model trained on an algorithmic task like modular addition first memorizes the training data and only much later, after many extra steps, suddenly generalizes to held-out inputs. Population-based training \(PBT\) is a hyperparameter optimization method that maintains a population of models with different hyperparameters and periodically copies weights or hyperparameters from better-performing members, allowing the training configuration to adapt over time. This write-up tests whether PBT can speed up or rescue grokking on a small modular addition task, which is relevant because grokking is known to be sensitive to hyperparameters such as learning rate and weight decay.

**「Impact」** Researchers should not expect PBT to accelerate grokking on workable hyperparameter ranges; the evidence indicates it can only salvage ranges where fixed settings fail, and wide random search reached 95% accuracy at a median of 300 steps versus about 6650 for PBT from a dead zone. If using PBT to search grokking hyperparameters, treat the learned schedule rather than weight inheritance as the mechanism, because replaying the schedule without PBT replicated success.
